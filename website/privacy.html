Privacy Policy

Short version: there's nothing to leak, because we don't collect anything.

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Dah Vinci does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No account is required to use the app.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind is included.
  • Your settings (speed, tone, input preferences) and your learning progress are stored only on your device using the operating system's standard local storage. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app makes no network connections to send your information.

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, sell, or disclose.

If Apple's App Store provides us aggregate, anonymized statistics about downloads, that data comes from Apple and is not linked to you by us.
