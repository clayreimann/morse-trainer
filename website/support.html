Support

Stuck on a drill, found a bug, or just want to say hi? We're glad to hear from you.

Dah Vinci is a Morse code learning app for iOS and macOS. It teaches you to send and copy real words, trains your ear with listen-and-copy drills, and gives you a playground to hear any text sent in Morse — with full control over speed, Farnsworth spacing, and tone.
